Generated by `node scripts/corpus-provenance.cjs --write` from this directory's `lemmas.json`.

License values are historical declarations, not legal conclusions. **declared** means a source was recorded;
this validation does not verify that source or its license. **verified** requires a repository evidence receipt
and human review; no entry is promoted to verified by this tool. **unresolved** means source evidence is missing.

need external evidence. No Commons URL is constructed from their explanatory text.
