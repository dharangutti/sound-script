# Sample media

`montage.mp4` is a six-second composed work made with the VideoLab renderer: two
trimmed user-supplied Pixabay downloads, a crossfade, an animated graphic and mixed
synthetic audio. It is not a redistribution of the original standalone downloads.
The supplied source filenames identify Pixabay videos 91 (coast footage) and
15305 (dog footage). No other personal recordings or private validation assets are
included. Source files remain under the ignored real-media directory.

Source: https://pixabay.com/videos/ · License information:
https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/

`pattern.mp4`, `calm.wav` and `pulse.wav` are generated test imagery and original
synthetic tones/chords created with FFmpeg for the Lab. They require no third-party
media download. All four small samples are versioned so the local workbench works
without the private source downloads. The composed montage is used as a media input
in `examples/showcase.json`; that composition adds independently controlled graphic
motion and audio gain.

These samples are for explaining composition mechanics. They are not a general
stock-media library. Public exports include the current composition and graphics.
